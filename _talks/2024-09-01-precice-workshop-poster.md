Poster, with Amitabh Bhattacharya. Awarded a travel grant to attend the workshop.

Two-dimensional immersed boundary simulations of cilia interacting with a particle, a neural network that detects the
particle's characteristics from the cilia tip deflections, and the next step: a 3D model coupling an OpenFOAM fluid
solver with a solid solver through preCICE.
