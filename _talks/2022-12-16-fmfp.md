Presented on 16 December 2022 at FMFP 2022, held at IIT Roorkee. Joint work with Amitabh Bhattacharya.

Simulations of particles carried over an array of passive artificial cilia, and an LSTM neural network that predicts the
particle's size, shape and position from how the cilia tips deflect.
