Superdroplet-based large eddy simulations of clouds usually assume each grid cell is well mixed, which misjudges droplet
growth near cloud edges. This preprint maps droplets in direct numerical simulation (DNS) data to superdroplets with a
new clustering algorithm, and trains a machine-learning model that relates their effective growth rate to filtered flow
variables.
